Short Circuit

Dang this mod blew up.

Short Circuit is a Minecraft redstone mod inspired by Compact Machines. It allows you to create compact, fast and modular redstone circuits. You can fit any redstone circuits as big as 256x256x256 blocks inside 1 block! It simply simulates the redstone circuit inside that 1 block, so it is compatible with most mods!

Download from CurseForge or Modrinth!

To get started, craft a Circuit and a Poking Stick. The Poking Stick allows you to initialize a circuit and enter its dimension. You can change the size of the circuit by right-clicking it against the air BEFORE entering the circuit. Inside the circuit, shift-right-click the wall the toggle its input and output state. Build your circuit, and exit the circuit by right-clicking the wall.

TADA! You have a dynamic circuit! You can then copy this circuit by right-clicking another circuit item on the circuit block. Placing these circuit items will create the same circuit but different instances.

A unique feature this mod offers is the Truth Assigner (a.k.a. the Washing Machine). Throw your dynamic circuit inside the machine, tweak some simulation settings and get back your BRAND NEW Integrated Circuit! Integrated Circuits (ICs) act like truth tables, any redstone signal will result in an instant out in the same tick. You can make fast computers with that.
